How it works

Four simple steps, all in cash

From application to payout — no bank account, no savings, no verification fee.

1

Apply online

Fill in your full name, email, phone number and ID/passport number. Upload a clear photo of your ID and a clear selfie.

2

Admin verification

Our admin team reviews your documents and loan request. You get a decision by phone or email.

3

Collect cash

Once approved, collect your cash at the Sarah Loans branch. Bring the original ID you uploaded.

4

Pay every saturday

Bring your weekly cash payment to the branch every Saturday until the loan is fully cleared.
